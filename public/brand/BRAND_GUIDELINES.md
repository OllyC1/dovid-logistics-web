# Dovid Logistics — Brand identity v1.0

## Brand idea
Delivering Trust. Moving Forward.

A scalable logistics brand, not tied to a specific city or transport mode.

## Core palette
- Primary navy: `#0B1F44`
- Velocity orange: `#FF6A00`
- Cloud white: `#F5F7FA`
- Body text: `#1F2937`

## Type
Montserrat ExtraBold / Bold for headlines and wordmark; Inter for UI and documents. SVG wordmarks contain editable text and require Montserrat installed for exact typography; convert text to outlines in a vector editor before print production.

## Mark
A geometric D ring with two forward speed strokes. Keep proportions unchanged. Minimum digital icon width: 24 px. Minimum horizontal logo width: 140 px. Clear space: at least 15% of mark height on all sides.

## Usage
Use primary on light backgrounds; reverse on navy; monochrome for single-color print. Never distort, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, add shadows, or put the logo over busy imagery without a solid backing.

## Applications
Rider uniforms, delivery boxes, helmets, fleet decals, waybills, website, tracking UI and social templates. Fleet and nationwide services are future-facing brand examples, not statements of current operating coverage.

## Production note
This is a first editable vector identity. Confirm font licensing, perform trademark/name clearance, and have a designer optically review curves and print proofs before large-scale production. No trademark registration is implied.
